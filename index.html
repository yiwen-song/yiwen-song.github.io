About News Publications Technical Reports

I am a Research Scientist at Google Cloud AI Research. My research focuses on Large Language Models, particularly at the intersection of multimodality, generative AI, and agentic systems. I am passionate about advancing how models reason, plan, and interact with complex, real-world environments.

Prior to joining Google, I spent four years at Meta conducting research and building training frameworks in the computer vision domain. I was a core contributor to Llama 3 and served as a maintainer for the TorchVision library.
